## Scope

This is a program design, not a post. It produces a launch schedule, a cadence
per participant, governance rules, and the metric definitions the program will be
judged by.

## Inputs to gather before designing

Team size, function mix, how much time each participant will actually give per
week, whether participation is voluntary, and what the company will and will not
approve. A program designed without the time budget fails in week two.

## Honesty rule

Do not promise a reach multiplier. If you quote a benchmark from the vendored
references, attribute it to the reference and say it is a benchmark, not a
forecast for this team.

## Note

Publishing is per-person: each participant's LinkedIn connection is separate, so
this skill plans and drafts, it does not mass-publish. Anything that goes out
still passes that person's own approval.
