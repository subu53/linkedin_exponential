## Why this skill matters more than it looks

It is the only skill that works for someone with no archive: the voice profile
needs posts already written, the Story Bank only needs a career. It is also the
supply line for every other skill, because it is where real numbers come from.
Without it, the other skills correctly refuse to invent figures and the drafts
stay vague.

## Interviewing rules

- **One question at a time.** Do not dump a questionnaire. The answers are only
  good if the operator thinks before replying.
- **Chase the number.** "We grew a lot" becomes useful only as a figure and a
  period. Ask until you have one, or record that none is available.
- **Record verbatim.** Put the operator's own phrasing in the bank; their words
  are the raw material, your paraphrase is not.
- **Mark the gaps.** A slot with no answer stays visibly empty rather than
  filled with a placeholder that later reads as fact.

## Writing to the bank

Update `../../vendor/references/story-bank.md` and set `filled: yes` when there
is real content. Tell the operator what was added. Never write a fabricated
receipt into the bank: every downstream draft treats it as true.
