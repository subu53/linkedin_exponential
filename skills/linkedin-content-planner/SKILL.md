## First question to ask

**Founder plan or general plan?** The founders edition swaps the pillar set
(Conviction / Building in public / The math / Proof) and draws on the 10 founder
angles. Ask before building the plan.

## What a usable plan contains

For each day: the topic, the format, the hook formula by code, the posting
window, and the specific comment targets. A plan that only names topics is not a
plan the operator can execute - tie every day to a formula and a goal.

## Inputs to gather

Role, ICP, and what they are selling or building. If a Story Bank is filled
(`../../vendor/references/story-bank.md` has `filled: yes`), read it and build
the week from the operator's actual receipts rather than generic advice.

## Honesty rule

Do not promise reach numbers you cannot support. Where a figure is quoted, name
it as the vendored reference's figure, not as a prediction.
